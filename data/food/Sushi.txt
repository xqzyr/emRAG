Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish that consists of vinegared rice combined with ingredients such as raw fish, vegetables, and seaweed.

There are many types of sushi, including nigiri, maki, and sashimi. Each type differs in preparation and presentation.

Freshness of ingredients is very important in sushi, especially when raw fish is used. High-quality fish and rice are essential for good sushi.

Sushi is often served with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger, which enhance the flavor of the dish.

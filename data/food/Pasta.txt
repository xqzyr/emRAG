Pasta is a staple food in Italian cuisine made from wheat and water, sometimes combined with eggs. It comes in many shapes and sizes.

Pasta is typically cooked by boiling and is often served with sauces such as tomato sauce, cream-based sauces, or pesto.

Common types of pasta include spaghetti, penne, and fusilli. Each type is suited to different kinds of sauces and dishes.

Pasta dishes can include a variety of ingredients such as vegetables, meat, and cheese, making it a versatile and widely consumed food.

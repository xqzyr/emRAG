Elephants are the largest land mammals on Earth and are known for their intelligence, memory, and strong social structures. They are native to Africa and Asia.

Elephants are herbivores and primarily consume grasses, fruits, bark, and leaves. Due to their large size, they require a significant amount of food and water each day.

These animals live in social groups led by a matriarch, typically the oldest and most experienced female. Elephants communicate using a variety of vocalizations and low-frequency sounds.

The average lifespan of an elephant is between 60 and 70 years, making them one of the longest-living land animals. They are also known for their strong emotional bonds and complex behaviors.

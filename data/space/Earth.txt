Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. It has a diverse environment that includes oceans, land, and an atmosphere.

The Earth's surface is composed of continents and oceans, with water covering approximately 70 percent of the planet.

Earth has a breathable atmosphere that contains oxygen, which is essential for most forms of life. It also has a protective magnetic field.

The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in approximately 365 days and rotates on its axis once every 24 hours.

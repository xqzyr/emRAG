Pizza is a savory dish originating from Italy, consisting of a flatbread base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.
The most traditional pizza is the Neapolitan pizza, made with tomatoes, mozzarella cheese, olive oil, and basil.
Modern pizzas include toppings such as pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, olives, and vegetables.
Pizza is baked at very high temperatures, traditionally in a wood-fired oven.
It is one of the most popular fast foods worldwide.

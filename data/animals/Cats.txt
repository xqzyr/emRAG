Cats are small domesticated mammals that are commonly kept as pets. They are known for their independence, agility, and ability to hunt small prey such as rodents.

The average lifespan of a domestic cat is typically between 12 and 18 years, which is generally longer than that of many dog breeds. Indoor cats often live longer than outdoor cats due to reduced exposure to risks.

Cats are obligate carnivores, meaning their diet primarily consists of meat. They have sharp claws and teeth that are adapted for hunting and capturing prey.

Unlike dogs, cats are generally less dependent on humans for social interaction, although they can still form strong bonds with their owners. They are often considered more independent and self-sufficient than dogs.

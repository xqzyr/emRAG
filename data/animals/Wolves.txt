Wolves are wild carnivorous mammals that belong to the canine family. They are closely related to domestic dogs and share many physical and behavioral characteristics.

Wolves typically live in structured social groups known as packs. These packs are often led by a dominant pair and rely on cooperation for hunting and survival.

The diet of wolves consists mainly of large herbivores such as deer and elk. They are highly skilled hunters and use coordinated strategies to capture prey.

Wolves generally have a lifespan of around 6 to 8 years in the wild, although they may live longer in protected environments. Unlike domesticated dogs, wolves are not adapted to living alongside humans.

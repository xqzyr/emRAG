A CPU, or central processing unit, is the primary processor in a computer responsible for executing instructions and managing system operations.

CPUs typically have a smaller number of powerful cores compared to GPUs. These cores are optimized for sequential processing and complex decision-making tasks.

CPUs are well suited for general-purpose computing, including running operating systems, handling user input, and managing applications. They are essential for tasks that require flexibility and control.

While CPUs can perform parallel operations to some extent, they are generally less efficient than GPUs for highly parallel workloads such as graphics rendering or deep learning.

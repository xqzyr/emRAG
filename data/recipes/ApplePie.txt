Apple Pie is a classic dessert made with apples, sugar, and spices baked in a pastry crust.
Traditional apple pie uses tart apples such as Granny Smith, combined with sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and sometimes lemon juice.
The filling is placed between a bottom and top crust, which can be a lattice or a full cover.
Apple pie is commonly served warm and is often paired with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
It is popular in many countries and is especially associated with American cuisine.

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system and is known as a gas giant. It is composed mainly of hydrogen and helium.

Jupiter has a very strong gravitational field and dozens of moons, including the four large Galilean moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto.

One of Jupiter's most notable features is the Great Red Spot, which is a massive storm that has been observed for centuries.

Jupiter does not have a solid surface like Earth and has a very thick atmosphere with powerful storms and high-speed winds.

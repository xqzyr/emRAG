Saturn is a gas giant known for its prominent ring system, which is composed of ice particles and rocky debris.

It is the second-largest planet in the solar system and, like Jupiter, is primarily made of hydrogen and helium.

Saturn has many moons, with Titan being the largest. Titan has a thick atmosphere and is of particular interest to scientists.

The planet has a low density compared to other planets, meaning it would float in water if such a large body of water existed.

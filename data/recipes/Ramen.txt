Ramen is a Japanese noodle soup dish consisting of wheat noodles served in a meat or fish-based broth.
Common broth types include soy sauce, miso, salt, and pork bone (tonkotsu).
Ramen is typically topped with sliced pork, soft-boiled eggs, seaweed, green onions, and bamboo shoots.
It originated from Chinese noodle soups and evolved into a staple of Japanese cuisine.
Ramen shops often specialize in specific broth styles and regional variations.

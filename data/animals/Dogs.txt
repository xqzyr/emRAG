Dogs are domesticated mammals that have lived alongside humans for thousands of years. They are known for their loyalty, intelligence, and ability to form strong social bonds with people.

The average lifespan of a dog typically ranges from 10 to 13 years, although this can vary depending on breed and size. Smaller dog breeds often live longer than larger breeds.

Dogs are omnivorous animals and commonly eat a combination of meat, grains, and specially formulated dog food. They have an extremely strong sense of smell, which is significantly more sensitive than that of humans.

In addition to being pets, dogs are widely used as working animals. They are trained for tasks such as search and rescue, guiding visually impaired individuals, herding livestock, and detecting substances.
